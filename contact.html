Support & feedback

For anything to do with our apps — a question, a bug, a feature you wish existed — email hello@monkeyfuse.com. We aim to respond within 24–48 hours on working days, though it can take a little longer during busy periods.

If you're reporting a bug, telling us which app and device you're using, and what you were doing at the time, helps us fix it far more quickly.

Privacy & data requests

To ask about our privacy practices, or to exercise your rights over your personal data — access, correction, deletion or portability — email hello@monkeyfuse.com. We respond to these requests within 30 days.

Our privacy policy sets out in detail what we collect, why, and how long we keep it.
