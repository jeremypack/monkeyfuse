About Us

Monkeyfuse is an independent software house dedicated to creating innovative mobile applications. We're not an agency - we build and launch our own products, focusing on apps that solve real problems and delight users.

Founded with a passion for mobile technology and product development, Monkeyfuse operates from the United Kingdom, bringing fresh perspectives to the app ecosystem. We take ideas from concept to launch, handling everything from design and development to marketing and ongoing support. Every app we create reflects our commitment to quality and user experience.

As a product-focused company, we have the freedom to experiment, iterate, and build exactly what we envision. We're constantly exploring new ideas and working on exciting projects that push the boundaries of what mobile apps can do.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create mobile applications that genuinely improve people's lives. We believe in building products that are not only functional and beautiful, but also intuitive and accessible. By focusing on our own products rather than client work, we can ensure that every decision prioritizes user value over short-term gains.

Our Values

  • User-Centric Design: Every feature we build starts with understanding user needs and pain points.
  • Quality Over Quantity: We'd rather build one exceptional app than ten mediocre ones.
  • Privacy First: We respect user privacy and only collect data that's essential for functionality.
  • Continuous Improvement: We iterate based on feedback and constantly refine our products.

What We Do

As a software house, we focus on building and launching our own mobile applications. Our end-to-end approach ensures that every aspect of our products meets our high standards for quality, usability, and innovation.

Product Development

We create native iOS applications from the ground up, using Swift and SwiftUI to build fast, beautiful, and reliable apps. Our development process emphasizes clean code, robust architecture, and thorough testing to ensure stability and maintainability.

Product Design

Every app we build features thoughtful, user-centered design that makes complex tasks simple and enjoyable. We follow Apple's Human Interface Guidelines while adding our own creative touches that make our apps stand out.

Product Launch

We handle the entire launch process, from App Store optimization to marketing, ensuring our apps reach the right audience. This includes App Store listing optimization, beta testing, and strategic marketing to maximize visibility.

Ongoing Support

We continuously improve our apps based on user feedback, adding new features and ensuring everything runs smoothly. Our commitment extends beyond launch, with regular updates, bug fixes, and feature enhancements.

Our Products

MasterMeals

MasterMeals is our first mobile application, currently in development and coming soon to the App Store. MasterMeals is designed to help users organize their recipes, plan meals, and create shopping lists effortlessly.

With MasterMeals, users can:

  • Save and organize their favorite recipes in one place
  • Import recipes from websites with a single tap
  • Plan weekly meals and generate shopping lists automatically
  • Modify recipes using AI-powered suggestions
  • Access their recipes and meal plans across all their devices

MasterMeals is built with privacy in mind. We don't use analytics, tracking, or advertising services. Your recipe data belongs to you, and we only collect what's necessary to provide the core functionality of the app.

MasterMeals will be available on the App Store soon. Stay tuned for updates!
