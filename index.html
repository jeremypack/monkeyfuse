Our work

MasterMeals

In review

Our first app. Your meals, mastered. MasterMeals keeps every recipe your family loves in one place, turns them into a simple weekly plan, and gives you one ready-to-shop list as you go.

It isn't another catalogue of recipes to scroll through. It's your household's own cookbook — built from the meals everyone actually says yes to, so answering "what's for dinner?" stops being a daily negotiation.

  • Save your keepers Build a personal cookbook from the recipes your household would happily eat again.
  • Every recipe has a home Import from links and social posts — including Instagram and TikTok — instead of losing good dinners to open tabs and screenshots.
  • Plan your week Drop trusted recipes into the days ahead, then move or swap anything as life changes.
  • Shop once, cook calmly Ingredients gather into one consolidated list as you plan, so a week's shop is a single trip.
  • Meet Chef Remy Ask what to prep ahead, make a recipe vegetarian, or adapt it for the freezer — and Remy updates the recipe to suit.
  • Scale without the maths Adjust servings and the ingredients follow.

Built without tracking

No analytics, no advertising SDKs and no cross-site tracking. We collect what the app needs to work, and nothing else — the only content that leaves your account is what you hand to Chef Remy, which goes to an AI provider to answer you. The MasterMeals privacy policy sets out exactly what that means.

MasterMeals is with the app stores for review — coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Visit mastermeals.app

About

Who we are

Monkeyfuse operates from Ashford, Kent, building products we want to exist.

Working on our own products rather than client work means we can take the time to get things right: to experiment, to throw away the version that didn't work, and to ship when it's ready rather than when it's billable.

The aim is straightforward — build mobile apps that are genuinely useful, pleasant to use, and honest about what they do with your data.
